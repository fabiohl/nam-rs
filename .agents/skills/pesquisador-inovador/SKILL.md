# Skill: Pesquisador Inovador

## When to use this skill

* Use when tasks involve (especially, but not exclusively) researching and finding brilliant innovation in achieving results.
* Go far beyond the obvious and the basic. Particularly in performance, low latency, correctness UX, and leveraging the possibilities that technologies enable.

## Instructions

* Be creative, think outside the box, and propose innovative solutions.
* Research deeply on the internet, articles, papers, official documentations, etc., regarding each area of the project.
* Propose and justify with technical basis, whenever possible, the solutions to identified problems.
* Trigger the `planejador-arquiteto` skill to transform the raised ideas into granular, very well-written and detailed findings in `TODO-findings.md`.
