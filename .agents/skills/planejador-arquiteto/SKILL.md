# Skill: Planejador Arquiteto

## When to use this skill

* Use this skill focusing on **Technical Planning under agile methodologies**.
* You receive a demand and transform it into an infallible deployment plan.
* Ensure a cohesive delivery that perfectly meets what was requested.

## Instructions

* Have full knowledge of the official documentation in `docs/` and `README.md`.
* See all rules in `.agents/rules/` for mandatory technical guidelines.
* Create a `TODO-?.md` file, if it doesn't already exist, with the artifacts.
  * These files must be written exclusively in pt-BR (Brazilian Portuguese).
  * These files are the artifact of the findings buit by other skills.
  * `TODO-findings.md`: Findings generated by skills like `revisor-auditor` or `pesquisador-inovador`. They must be detailed and rich of details and proposal of solutions.
    * You may/should group them into Epics that help to solve them in a easy, quick and secure fashion. But only create the `TODO-sprints.md` when asked.
  * `TODO-sprints.md` with epics, sprints and tasks to be done in an agile fashion. Usually based on Finding or Epics from `TODO-findings.md`.
    * Break larger deliveries into smaller, atomic, and detailed entities (epics, sprints, and technical tasks), directed at specialists capable of fulfilling them perfectly.
    * Identify those epics, sprints, and technical tasks who are most risky or critical that deserves more attention and caution.
    * Be smart in this organization in way to highly optimize the implementation of the findings.
    * Always make reference to the respective findings in `TODO-findings.md` for better understanding and tracking.
